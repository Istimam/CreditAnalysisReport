ℹ️ How to read this Credit Customer Report (Accounting Definitions) ▼ Expand

Accounting Core Rule: Accounts must never confuse customer credit balances with returns. Credit Sold, Credit Returned, and Net Credit are always calculated and presented side-by-side:

Credit Sold
The amount of the invoice that was placed on credit at the exact time of sale.
Total Return
Value of all products returned/exchanged on the invoice (both cash & credit portions).
Credit Returned
The return portion that specifically offsets credit owed (never exceeds Credit Sold).
Net Credit
Credit Sold − Credit Returned. True debt generated by sales.
Collected
Money received from customer against net credit (Cash, Bank, Wallet, Deductions).
Credit Due
Net Credit − Collected. The true outstanding receivable balance.
Net Sale
Gross − Discount − Total Return ± Round Off. Actual sales revenue recognized.
