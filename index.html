ℹ How to read this report & Accounting Definitions ▾
Credit Sale: Credit portion of invoices listed in this report.
Credit Return: Credit reduced by returns/exchanges attributed to their ORIGINAL sale invoice.
Net Credit Sale: Credit Sale − Credit Return.
Total Collection: Money received against listed invoices.
Net Due: What is still unpaid on the listed invoices.
Outstanding Due: Total unpaid credit across ALL invoices of all time as of today (04-11-2026). Unaffected by report filters.
Available Credit: Credit Limit − Outstanding Due (red if negative = over limit).
Credit Limit: The customer's approved maximum credit ceiling.
Worked Example: Limit 10,000. October invoices: Credit Sale 6,000, Credit Return 500, Net Credit Sale 5,500. Collected 2,000, Net Due 3,500. Older unpaid 1,200 from September, so Outstanding Due = 4,700, Available Credit = 5,300, Credit Limit = 10,000.
